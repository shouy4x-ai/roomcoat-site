Price a paint job before you leave the walk-through.

RoomCoat is an iPhone app for solo painters and small crews. Measure the rooms, set your prices, attach photos, and share a professional estimate as a PDF.

What it does

  • Measure rooms, doors, and windows, then get square feet and gallons to buy.
  • Set a final job price, or add your own sections and line items.
  • Attach photos from the camera or your library so the customer can see the work.
  • Share a PDF from the share sheet you already use.

Estimates stay on your device. RoomCoat has no account, and it does not text or email your clients for you.

RoomCoat Pro

You can keep a few of your own estimates for free. RoomCoat Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription, sold through the App Store, for unlimited estimates on that device, unlimited photos, your logo on PDFs, saved pricing presets, and PDFs without the RoomCoat line. The App Store shows the price and any free trial you qualify for.
