Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

RoomCoat is a painting estimate app published by Beshouy Mikhael. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device. It is written in plain language so you can see what stays local and what is handled by Apple.

Your estimates stay on your device

RoomCoat does not create an account, and it does not run a RoomCoat server that receives your estimates. Business details, client details, room measurements, prices, rates, notes, signatures, and photos you attach are stored locally in the app on that iPhone or iPad.

If you back up the device with iCloud or a computer, that backup may include the app’s local files as part of your own backup. That backup is controlled by you and by Apple, not by a RoomCoat website.

Photos and camera

RoomCoat asks for camera and photo library access only so you can attach pictures to an estimate and, if you choose, include them on the PDF you generate. Photos are not uploaded to a RoomCoat server. They leave the device only if you share the estimate or the photos yourself, for example through the iOS share sheet.

Purchases

RoomCoat Pro subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). RoomCoat does not receive your payment card number, and it does not see your Apple ID password. Restore Purchases uses Apple’s purchase records for your Apple ID. Apple’s privacy policy applies to how Apple processes those purchases.

No tracking

RoomCoat does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. It does not use advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, or third-party ad networks. The app’s privacy manifest declares that it does not track and that it does not collect data for a developer server.

When you share a PDF

If you export or share an estimate, the PDF includes the business, client, pricing, and photo information you chose to put on it. Where that file goes — Messages, Mail, Files, a printer, or another app — is your choice, using the system share sheet.

Children

RoomCoat is built for trade professionals. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about the information stored in your copy of the app, can be sent to shouy4x@gmail.com.
